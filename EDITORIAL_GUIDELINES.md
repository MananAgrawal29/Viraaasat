# Viraaasat — Editorial Guidelines

## Voice
Thoughtful, candid, curious, cinematic, evidence-minded, human.

Write like a documentary narrator with an intelligent editorial voice—not like an academic paper, marketing copy, government brochure, influencer, or political spokesperson.

## Editorial posture
- Neither blindly patriotic nor reflexively negative.
- Don't flatten India into a single experience.
- Show regional, linguistic, class, caste, religious, gender, urban/rural, generational and other differences where relevant and responsibly sourced.
- Present difficult realities without sensationalism.
- Present achievements without exaggeration.
- Be clear about uncertainty, disagreement and limitations.
- Avoid false balance: weight claims according to evidence, not simply opposing sides.

## Storytelling
Stories should have a strong question, tension, reveal, or narrative arc.
A story may move between:
- a human moment
- place and context
- history
- evidence/data
- competing perspectives
- consequences
- reflection or open question

Do not force every story to follow the same order. Let the subject determine the structure.

## Writing
- Clear, vivid, precise prose.
- Define unfamiliar terms.
- Use short paragraphs and purposeful headings.
- Avoid empty dramatic language.
- Avoid unsupported superlatives.
- Do not make facts sound more certain than the source allows.
- Keep headlines intriguing but accurate.
- Captions add context rather than repeat obvious information.

## Research and sourcing
- Cite important factual claims.
- Use primary sources where possible.
- Cross-check consequential claims.
- Distinguish reporting, analysis, opinion, official claims, allegations and estimates.
- Include methodology for data-based stories.
- Record update dates and corrections.

## Ethics
- Avoid stereotypes and dehumanizing descriptions.
- Protect vulnerable sources and private individuals.
- Consider safety, consent and context when depicting people.
- Use sensitive imagery responsibly.
- Avoid misleading cropping, manipulative timelines, or charts that exaggerate.
- Disclose uncertainty and editorial limitations.

## Visual narrative
Visuals, maps, charts, sound and motion should clarify, contextualize or deepen the story. If an effect does not serve the story, remove it.
