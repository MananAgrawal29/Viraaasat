# Viraaasat — Design System

**Status:** Brand direction locked; exact implementation tokens and logo remain open for refinement.

## Design character
Premium editorial × archival heritage × cinematic documentary × interactive museum.

Keywords: thoughtful, candid, curious, rooted, contemporary, tactile, atmospheric, intellectual, human.

The design should feel like an archive of a living civilization—not a nostalgic museum frozen in time.

## Locked palette
| Token | Hex | Intended use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink / Charcoal | `#191B18` | Immersive dark sections, text, contrast |
| Archive Ivory | `#F1EBDD` | Main editorial surfaces, reading backgrounds |
| Terracotta | `#B66A46` | Human stories, culture, warm accents |
| Forest | `#68745C` | Ecology, geography, quiet secondary accents |
| Old Brass | `#C6A66B` | Timeline markers, metadata, details |
| Oxide Red | `#7D3430` | Conflict, difficult histories, serious accents |

Use restraint. A page or story may have an accent color, but do not deploy every palette color at once. Maintain readable contrast.

## Locked type pairing
- **Display / headlines:** Cormorant Garamond
- **Body / UI:** Manrope
- **Numbers / data / labels:** IBM Plex Mono

Use hierarchy and line length deliberately. Typography should feel editorial, not like a generic dashboard.

## Logo
**OPEN / undecided.** Minimal editorial/archival mark preferred.
Potential exploration: distinctive wordmark, restrained V/archival shape, or subtle script/inscription reference.
Avoid default India-map silhouette, flag, Ashoka Chakra, lotus, mandala, or clichéd patriotic graphics.

## Imagery
- Documentary, observed, and grounded.
- Real landscapes, cities, architecture, archival material, ordinary people, details, and atmosphere.
- Natural light, tactile surfaces, subtle grain where appropriate, wide establishing shots balanced with intimate details.
- Avoid generic stock-photo compositions, staged tourism imagery, excessive saturation, and visual clichés.
- Image treatment must not conceal or romanticize the reality of a story.

## Motion
Motion is an identity feature, not decoration.
- Scroll-driven, intentional, cinematic.
- Slow for reflection; sharp for revelation; restrained for information.
- Use reveals, parallax, chapter transitions, image wipes, map movement, timeline progression, count-ups, and text choreography when appropriate.
- Do not animate everything.
- No scroll hijacking.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Keep text readable if animation fails or is disabled.
- Provide alternatives for pinned/complex experiences on mobile and reduced-motion modes.

## Homepage direction (decisions from initial discussion)
- Dominant feeling: combine awe, curiosity, emotional connection, intrigue, and intellectual fascination.
- Opening: a cinematic opening that evolves into an interactive homepage.
- Homepage structure: a long, orchestrated cinematic journey.
- India identity: subtle and bold in unconventional ways; avoid cliché symbols, use place, texture, language, typography, and storytelling.
- Style blend: A24/cinematic, refined editorial, digital museum, and experimental creative studio. The blend is intentional; do not reduce it to one reference.

## Homepage elements / concepts
- A cinematic hero and identity introduction.
- A featured story with strong visual presence.
- An invitation to explore India through themes, places, eras, or questions.
- A story archive / discovery system.
- A visible way to access “This Week’s Story”.
- Social links: GitHub and Instagram initially; other platforms may be added later.
- A compact footer with project identity, credits, sources or about information as appropriate.

## Responsive principles
- Mobile is not a shrunk desktop layout.
- Recompose sections and interactions for touch.
- Avoid hover-only discovery.
- Keep controls reachable and readable.
- Ensure cinematic effects do not cause excessive battery, heat, or data use.
