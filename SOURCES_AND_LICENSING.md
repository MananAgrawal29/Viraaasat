# Viraaasat — Sources, Citations & Media Licensing

## Research hierarchy (guideline, not absolute)
1. Primary documents, original datasets, laws, judgments, official records, interviews, direct observation.
2. Peer-reviewed scholarship, academic books, credible research institutions.
3. Established journalism with transparent reporting and editorial standards.
4. Specialist/credible explainers used for orientation and cross-checking.
5. Social posts and crowdsourced sources only with careful verification and contextual use.

Official sources are not automatically correct or complete; evaluate methodology, incentives, definitions and limitations.

## Citation record for claims
For each key claim, retain:
- Claim / text being supported
- Source title and author/organization
- URL or archival identifier
- Publication date
- Dataset's period/date and geographic scope
- Access date
- Relevant page/table/section
- Evidence notes and caveats
- Confidence / cross-check status

## Asset ledger
For every published image/video/audio/graphic:
- Asset ID and filename
- Creator/photographer
- Original source URL
- License name and link
- Required attribution text
- Date obtained
- Allowed usage and restrictions
- Modifications made
- Story/section placement
- Proof or copy of license where practical

## Rules
- Never assume content is free to reuse because it is publicly visible.
- Never remove watermarks or evade rights restrictions.
- Do not use AI-generated media to fabricate documentary evidence.
- AI-generated illustrative visuals must be clearly labeled when their context could mislead.
- For archival media, preserve the source and context.
- Check third-party map tile/data licenses and attribution.
